The concept

European gastronomy,
told with a Dhaka accent.

SINCE 2022

“Every dish is built from pristine ingredients and finished at the table’s pace, not the kitchen’s.”

Set in the heart of Banani, Segreto is a fine-dining room built for romantic evenings, quiet celebrations, and dinners that run long. Delicate artisanal starters, slow-cooked ragùs, wood-fired pizzas, and a wide-ranging Japanese counter share the same kitchen — sophisticated contemporary décor, plush booth seating, and mood lighting hold it all together.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Do you offer home delivery?

Yes — we deliver via Foodpanda, Pathao, and Uber Eats across Dhaka. You can also order directly via WhatsApp at +880 1977-197254 for the fastest service.

Can I pay with bKash or Nagad?

Yes! We accept bKash and Nagad for your convenience alongside cash, Visa, Mastercard, and AMEX. Mobile banking is fully supported at Segreto.

Is the food Halal certified?

Yes, our entire kitchen operates under Halal standards. All meat and poultry used at Segreto is certified Halal, sourced from trusted local and international suppliers.

Can I make a reservation for a large group?

Absolutely. For parties of 9 or more, we offer private dining options. Use the reservation form above and note your party size, or call us directly.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Our kitchen accommodates vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, and halal requests. Please inform us at the time of reservation or when ordering.

Do you host private events and corporate dinners?

Yes, Segreto offers dedicated private spaces for birthdays, anniversaries, corporate events, and celebrations. Contact us for bespoke event packages.

Is there parking available?

We offer valet parking for diners. The restaurant is also within walking distance of major Banani landmarks, and rideshare drop-off is convenient.
